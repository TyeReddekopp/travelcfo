Privacy Policy

TravelCFO  ·  Last updated: May 2026

The short version: TravelCFO stores your data on your device only. We do not collect, sell, or share your personal information. When you tap affiliate links, our partners track those clicks — that is disclosed below.

1. Who We Are

TravelCFO is a travel cost planning app developed and operated by Tye Reddekopp ("we", "us", or "our"). If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at tareddekopp@shaw.ca.

2. Information We Collect

2a. Information stored locally on your device

The following data is saved using your device's local storage (AsyncStorage). It never leaves your device and is never sent to our servers:

  • Trip preferences — destination selections, travel dates, party size, travel style, and duration
  • Points balances — loyalty program balances you enter manually (e.g. Aeroplan, Chase, Amex)
  • Saved trips — trips you choose to save within the app
  • App settings — your preferred currency, departure city, and display preferences

This data exists only on your phone. If you delete the app, this data is permanently deleted.

2b. Location data

If you grant permission, TravelCFO uses your device's GPS to detect your departure city. This is used solely to estimate flight costs from your location to your chosen destination. Your precise location is processed on-device and is not transmitted to our servers. You can deny location access and enter your city manually at any time.

2c. Information we do NOT collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Your name, email address, or any account information
  • Payment information
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Crash reports or analytics data
  • Browsing history or usage patterns

3. AI-Powered Features

TravelCFO uses the Google Gemini API to generate AI-powered cost estimates. When you request an AI estimate, your trip details (destination, month, duration, party size, travel style, and departure city) are sent to Google's servers to generate a response. No personally identifying information is included in these requests. Google's use of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

You can use the app with static estimates only by tapping the "📊 Static" toggle on the Cost screen — this does not send any data to Google.

4. Affiliate Links and Third-Party Booking Partners

TravelCFO contains affiliate links to third-party travel services including Booking.com, GetYourGuide, Viator, Hostelworld, Skyscanner, Expedia, World Nomads, SafetyWing, iVisa, and others. When you tap these links:

  • You are directed to the third-party website or app
  • The third party may place a cookie or use other tracking methods to attribute the referral
  • If you complete a booking, we may receive a commission at no additional cost to you
  • The third party's own privacy policy governs any data they collect

We use Travelpayouts (marker: 730194) as our affiliate tracking platform. Their privacy policy is available at travelpayouts.com/privacy-policy.

5. Credit Card Recommendations

TravelCFO displays credit card recommendations with application links. These links may be affiliate links through programs operated by Ratehub.ca, NerdWallet, or individual card issuers. We do not collect any information about your credit card applications or financial situation. Card recommendations are based on the travel programs and countries we have data for, not on your personal financial profile.

6. Subscriptions

TravelCFO is currently free to use. When subscription features are enabled in a future update, payments will be processed by Apple through the App Store. We will not have access to your payment details. Subscription management will be handled by RevenueCat. Their privacy policy is available at revenuecat.com/privacy.

7. Email List

If you have signed up for updates through our website or landing page, your email address is stored by the form provider you used (e.g. Notion, Mailchimp). We use this solely to send launch updates and product announcements. You can unsubscribe at any time by replying to any email or contacting us directly.

8. Children's Privacy

TravelCFO is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through our app, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

9. Your Rights

For users in the European Economic Area (GDPR)

Since TravelCFO does not collect personal data on our servers, there is no data for us to provide, correct, or delete. If you have signed up for our email list, you have the right to access, correct, or delete that data by contacting us.

For California residents (CCPA)

We do not sell personal information. Since we do not collect personal data on our servers, there is no data subject to CCPA access or deletion requests related to the app itself.

10. Data Security

Because all app data is stored locally on your device, the security of your data is protected by your device's built-in security features (PIN, Face ID, Touch ID). We recommend keeping your device's operating system updated and using a secure lock screen.

11. Third-Party Links

Our app contains links to third-party websites and services. This privacy policy applies only to TravelCFO. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of any third-party sites or services you access through our app.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
