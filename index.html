This Privacy Policy describes how Tye Reddekopp, doing business as TravelCFO ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares your personal information when you use the TravelCFO mobile application.

Questions? Contact us at support@travelcfo.com.

1. What Information Do We Collect?

Information you provide directly

  • Name (first and last) — provided at account creation
  • Email address — used to create and authenticate your account
  • Password — used to secure your account (stored encrypted by Supabase; we never see it in plaintext)
  • Country — used to personalise currency defaults and destination suggestions
  • Age confirmation — you confirm you are 13 or older at signup

Information collected automatically when you use the app

  • Location (optional) — if you grant permission, we detect your approximate location once to identify your nearest departure city for flight cost estimates. We do not store your precise coordinates.
  • Photos / camera (optional) — if you use the "Scan Screenshot" or "Take Photo" feature in the Trip Planner, the image is sent to Anthropic's API for price extraction and is not stored by us.
  • Trip and itinerary data — the destinations, costs, and trip plans you enter are stored in your account so they sync across your devices.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect phone numbers, mailing addresses, payment card details, browsing history, advertising identifiers, or any behavioural or analytics data.

2. How Do We Use Your Information?

  • To provide the service — your name and country personalise the app; your email and password authenticate your account; your trip data is synced to your account.
  • To send account emails — Supabase sends transactional emails (email confirmation, password reset) on our behalf. We do not send marketing emails.
  • To power AI features — booking screenshots or photos you submit are sent to Anthropic for price extraction only. They are not stored or used to train models.

3. When and With Whom Do We Share Your Information?

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with advertisers or data brokers. We share data only with the following service providers who process it on our behalf:

Service Purpose Privacy Policy
Supabase Database, authentication, and cloud sync supabase.com/privacy
Anthropic AI price extraction from booking screenshots anthropic.com/privacy
RevenueCat Subscription management revenuecat.com/privacy
Apple (App Store) In-app purchases and payment processing apple.com/legal/privacy

We may also share information if required by law or in connection with a business transfer (merger, acquisition, or sale of assets), in which case we will notify you.

4. Artificial Intelligence Features

The Trip Planner includes an optional feature that lets you photograph or upload a booking confirmation screenshot. The image is sent to Anthropic's Claude API which extracts the price, transport mode, and route. The image is not stored by us or used to train AI models.

This feature is entirely opt-in. You can always enter prices manually instead.

5. Payments

All subscription purchases are processed through Apple's in-app purchase system. We never see or store your payment card number or billing address. RevenueCat receives a subscription receipt from Apple to verify your entitlement.

For details, see:

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy

6. How Long Do We Keep Your Information?

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your data is removed from our systems. Booking screenshots processed by the AI feature are not retained at all — they are discarded immediately after processing.

7. How Do We Keep Your Information Safe?

Your data is stored in Supabase, which encrypts data at rest and in transit and enforces row-level security so that each user can only access their own data. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plaintext.

No method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. While we use commercially reasonable measures to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your personal information
  • Withdraw consent at any time (where processing is based on consent)
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

How to exercise your rights: You can update your name and country directly in the app (tap your avatar → Edit). To delete your account or request a copy of your data, email support@travelcfo.com. We will respond within 30 days.

9. US State Privacy Rights

Residents of California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, and other US states with privacy laws may have additional rights, including the right to know what personal information we collect, the right to delete it, and the right to opt out of its sale (we do not sell personal information).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@travelcfo.com. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.

10. Other Regions (EU, UK, Canada)

European Union and United Kingdom (GDPR)

If you are located in the EU or UK, our legal bases for processing your personal information are:

  • Performance of a contract — processing your name, email, and country to provide the service you signed up for.
  • Legitimate interests — sending transactional account emails.
  • Consent — accessing your location or camera (you may withdraw consent at any time in your device settings).

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or port your personal data, and to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.

Canada (PIPEDA / Law 25)

Canadian residents may request access to or correction of their personal information, or withdraw consent to its collection, by contacting us at support@travelcfo.com.
