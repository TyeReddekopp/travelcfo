Why TravelCFO is different

Built for how financially-minded
travellers actually think

  • ✓
    It's a planning tool, not a booking tool
    TravelCFO helps you decide before you book, not after. No other app models the complete cost of a trip before you've committed to anything.
  • ✓
    Points are treated as real currency
    Most travel apps ignore your loyalty balances entirely. TravelCFO converts them to dollar values and subtracts them from your total — giving you the number that actually matters.
  • ✓
    Timing is shown as a financial decision
    Going to Barcelona in November instead of July saves over $1,200. TravelCFO makes that visible at a glance so you can act on it.
  • ✓
    Prices adjust for where you live
    A flight from Edmonton costs very differently than one from London or Sydney. TravelCFO estimates costs from your city, not a generic "North American" baseline.
